FAQs 

Q1: What are interchange fees and who sets them in Canada? 

A: Interchange is part of the cost of accepting cards and is associated with payments to card issuers under card-network rules. The applicable rates depend on the network and transaction characteristics. 

Q2: How does a payment processor mark up interchange fees for Canadian merchants? 

A: A processor may add a percentage markup, per-transaction fee, monthly fee or other charges above underlying interchange and network costs, depending on the pricing agreement. 

Q3: What is the difference between a flat-rate and interchange-plus pricing model? 

A: Flat-rate pricing bundles costs into a simpler stated rate, while interchange-plus shows underlying interchange separately and adds the processor’s agreed markup.
