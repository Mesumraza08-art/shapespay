01

Overview

ShapesPay is a US & Canadian payment technology provider built on more than 15 years of industry expertise. We deliver innovative, user-friendly payment solutions at competitive rates, simplifying how merchants accept payments and reducing the cost of doing business. Explore our services to see how ShapesPay makes payments smarter and more efficient.

02

Why select us?

At ShapesPay, we strive to be more than just a service provider. We aim to be a trusted partner for the merchants we serve, offering unwavering support and transparency. Our approach focuses on delivering honest and straightforward payment solutions that not only save business owners money but also foster trust and collaboration.

03

How do we help?

Our role is to empower business owners to run their operations their way while we take care of payment processing seamlessly. We prioritize safety, reliability, and innovation, offering integrated tools designed to boost profitability. At ShapesPay, we understand that your business never takes a break, so our products are built to work around the clock. We are committed to being the payment partner that matches your dedication, ensuring every sale is smooth, secure, and successful.
