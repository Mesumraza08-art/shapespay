About ShapesPay

What is ShapesPay and how does it help my business?

ShapesPay is an all-in-one payment and POS solution designed to streamline transactions, reduce costs, and enhance customer experience. It offers secure, affordable, and fast payment processing for businesses of all sizes — from small retailers to large enterprises.

How does the ShapesPay POS system work?

 ShapesPay POS works in four simple steps:

  1. Sign Up – Create your ShapesPay account in minutes.
  2. Set Up – Connect your POS hardware or virtual terminal.
  3. Transact – Accept secure, fast, and contactless payments.
  4. Get Paid – Receive settlements quickly in your bank account
What makes ShapesPay different from other POS providers?

ShapesPay stands out for its low transaction fees, fast settlements, easy system integration, top-tier security, and 24/7 customer support. You keep more profits, process payments faster, and get round-the-clock assistance when needed.

Is ShapesPay POS suitable for my business type?

Absolutely. ShapesPay is ideal for retail, hospitality, healthcare, home services, auto repair, and professional service businesses. It scales easily as your operations grow and supports both in-store and remote payments.

What payment methods does ShapesPay support?

ShapesPay supports all major payment types, including credit cards, debit cards, contactless (tap), and mobile wallets. Its virtual terminal also enables remote payments over the phone or online — perfect for service providers or small businesses without physical POS hardware.

How secure is ShapesPay for customer and business data?

ShapesPay uses advanced encryption, secure servers, and 3D Secure (3DS) authentication to safeguard all transactions. Regular system updates and compliance with PCI standards ensure your business and customers remain protected from cyber threats.

Does ShapesPay include reporting and analytics tools?

Yes. ShapesPay offers real-time sales reporting, analytics dashboards, and detailed transaction tracking. These insights help you monitor performance, identify best-selling products, and make data-driven business decisions.

Can I manage my inventory and employees with ShapesPay POS?

Definitely. The POS system includes inventory management to track stock automatically with every sale and employee management tools to monitor hours, sales, and performance. It’s a complete operations solution — not just a payment processor.

What is the ShapesPay Virtual Terminal and who can use it?

The Virtual Terminal is a browser-based payment tool that allows you to process transactions remotely without needing a physical POS machine. It’s ideal for consultants, freelancers, non-profits, and service providers who accept payments by phone, email, or online invoice.

How do I get started with ShapesPay?

Getting started is simple:

  • Visit shapespay.com and click “Sign Up”
  • Complete a short onboarding form
  • Connect your POS system or virtual terminal
  • Start accepting payments within minutes

Our support team is available 24/7 to help you integrate, train your staff, and ensure smooth setup.

POS Solution

Questions merchants ask us

What's included in the ShapesPay POS bundle?

The full bundle pairs the D3 smart register (with its built-in receipt printer), a wireless Moneris Go Plus terminal, and an integrated Moneris Go PIN Pad — all connected to your ShapesPay merchant portal. Onboarding, staff training and 24/7 support are part of every setup.

Can I start with just a terminal and add the register later?

Absolutely. The system is modular — many merchants start with a single wireless terminal and add the register, PIN pad, or extra terminals as they grow. Everything you add reports into the same portal.

Which payment types can I accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Interac debit — by tap, chip insert, or swipe — plus mobile wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay. Card-not-present sales for phone and mail orders are supported through manual entry, and gift card programs are available.

Do the devices need an internet connection?

Yes — the register and PIN pad connect over your Wi-Fi or wired Ethernet, while the Go Plus terminal runs on 4G cellular out of the box and switches to Wi-Fi automatically whenever your network signal is stronger. That means the wireless terminal keeps working even if your store internet drops.

How do refunds and end-of-day work?

Refunds and voids take a few taps from the Reports screen on the terminal — you can even scan the QR code on a receipt to pull up the exact transaction. Your batch closes automatically each night, funds settle to your business account, and totals appear in your portal reporting.

How long does setup take?

Most merchants are live within days. We pre-configure your hardware, help build your product catalog, connect the devices to your network, and train your team — then stay available around the clock once you're selling.

What are my payment options for POS plans?

Hardware is a simple monthly rental, per device, billed monthly — no long lock-in, cancel anytime. Software and processing are quoted to fit your business, and bundled setups can be combined on a single bill.

Are there any upfront costs?

No hidden setup fees. Because hardware is rented monthly, there's no large equipment purchase up front. If your setup needs custom work, we quote it clearly before you commit to anything.

How much does support cost?

Nothing extra. Onboarding, staff training and 24/7 phone, email and chat support are included with every ShapesPay plan — you're our customer, so we look after you.

Can I use my own card processor?

ShapesPay handles your card and Interac processing end-to-end on Moneris — one of Canada's payment leaders — with transparent rates quoted to your monthly volume. That's what keeps your POS, payments and reporting together in one place, on one statement.

Will it work for franchises or multiple locations?

Yes. The merchant portal is built for multi-location businesses — switch between locations, manage menus and users centrally, and see consolidated reports across every terminal and store from one dashboard.

Interchanges FAQ

Interchanges FAQ page
What is MDR?

MDR (Merchant Discount Rate) is the fee charged to a merchant for processing card transactions. It is made up of several components, including interchange or wholesale discount fees, card brand assessments, and processing, settlement, and reporting costs.

What is an Interchange Rate?

Interchange is the fee that card processors—such as Moneris Solutions—pay to credit card issuers or financial institutions for each credit card transaction. These rates are set and regulated by the card networks, including Visa and Mastercard.

What is a Wholesale Discount Rate?

The Wholesale Discount Rate is the fee that card processors—such as Moneris Solutions—pay to card issuers or financial institutions for each transaction. These rates are established by American Express.

Where can I find information on interchange fees and rate levels?

Merchants are encouraged to visit the official card brand websites for detailed and up-to-date information on interchange fees. This includes Visa, Mastercard, Interac, Discover, and American Express.

When my rate changes, do I need to enter a new contract term?

No. Any rate adjustments do not affect the terms and conditions of your existing merchant agreement. Your contract term remains unchanged.
