All FAQs
What is ShapesPay and how does it help my business?

ShapesPay is an all-in-one payment and POS solution designed to streamline transactions, reduce costs, and enhance customer experience. It offers secure, affordable, and fast payment processing for businesses of all sizes — from small retailers to large enterprises.

How does the ShapesPay POS system work?

 ShapesPay POS works in four simple steps:

  1. Sign Up – Create your ShapesPay account in minutes.
  2. Set Up – Connect your POS hardware or virtual terminal.
  3. Transact – Accept secure, fast, and contactless payments.
  4. Get Paid – Receive settlements quickly in your bank account
What makes ShapesPay different from other POS providers?

ShapesPay stands out for its low transaction fees, fast settlements, easy system integration, top-tier security, and 24/7 customer support. You keep more profits, process payments faster, and get round-the-clock assistance when needed.

Is ShapesPay POS suitable for my business type?

Absolutely. ShapesPay is ideal for retail, hospitality, healthcare, home services, auto repair, and professional service businesses. It scales easily as your operations grow and supports both in-store and remote payments.

What payment methods does ShapesPay support?

ShapesPay supports all major payment types, including credit cards, debit cards, contactless (tap), and mobile wallets. Its virtual terminal also enables remote payments over the phone or online — perfect for service providers or small businesses without physical POS hardware.

How secure is ShapesPay for customer and business data?

ShapesPay uses advanced encryption, secure servers, and 3D Secure (3DS) authentication to safeguard all transactions. Regular system updates and compliance with PCI standards ensure your business and customers remain protected from cyber threats.

Does ShapesPay include reporting and analytics tools?

Yes. ShapesPay offers real-time sales reporting, analytics dashboards, and detailed transaction tracking. These insights help you monitor performance, identify best-selling products, and make data-driven business decisions.

Can I manage my inventory and employees with ShapesPay POS?

Definitely. The POS system includes inventory management to track stock automatically with every sale and employee management tools to monitor hours, sales, and performance. It’s a complete operations solution — not just a payment processor.

What is the ShapesPay Virtual Terminal and who can use it?

The Virtual Terminal is a browser-based payment tool that allows you to process transactions remotely without needing a physical POS machine. It’s ideal for consultants, freelancers, non-profits, and service providers who accept payments by phone, email, or online invoice.

How do I get started with ShapesPay?

Getting started is simple:

  • Visit shapespay.com and click “Sign Up”
  • Complete a short onboarding form
  • Connect your POS system or virtual terminal
  • Start accepting payments within minutes

Our support team is available 24/7 to help you integrate, train your staff, and ensure smooth setup.
