FAQ

What is MDR?

MDR (Merchant Discount Rate) is the fee charged to a merchant for processing card transactions. It is made up of several components, including interchange or wholesale discount fees, card brand assessments, and processing, settlement, and reporting costs.

What is an Interchange Rate?

Interchange is the fee that card processors—such as Moneris Solutions—pay to credit card issuers or financial institutions for each credit card transaction. These rates are set and regulated by the card networks, including Visa and Mastercard.

What is a Wholesale Discount Rate?

The Wholesale Discount Rate is the fee that card processors—such as Moneris Solutions—pay to card issuers or financial institutions for each transaction. These rates are established by American Express.

Where can I find information on interchange fees and rate levels?

Merchants are encouraged to visit the official card brand websites for detailed and up-to-date information on interchange fees. This includes Visa, Mastercard, Interac, Discover, and American Express.

When my rate changes, do I need to enter a new contract term?

No. Any rate adjustments do not affect the terms and conditions of your existing merchant agreement. Your contract term remains unchanged.

Questions about your rate?

Talk to a payment specialist about interchange, wholesale discount rates and your merchant agreement.
