Your taps rotate constantly — a seasonal IPA kicks at 7pm, a new sour goes on, a collaboration drops mid-shift. ShapesPay was built for that reality. Flexible Formats

Flights, Pints, Growlers, Crowlers, Cans.

Selling beer isn't selling burgers. The same hazy IPA might leave the bar as a 4oz taster, a 10oz pour, a 16oz pint, a flight component, a 32oz crowler, a 64oz growler fill, a 4-pack of cans, or a case to-go — each with its own price and inventory deduction. ShapesPay handles every format from a single tap, deducts ounces accurately, and keeps your cost-of-goods reporting honest.

  • Flexible pricing — taster, pour, pint, flight, growler, crowler
  • Flight builder — guests pick four, the POS prices it
  • Ounces deduct from the keg automatically per pour
  • Cans, 4-packs, cases, and merch ring through the same till
