Multi-Language

Servers In English. Kitchen In Korean.

ShapesPay makes it easy for wait staff to take orders in English and route them to the kitchen in Korean automatically. You can also opt to have servers enter orders by picture from a visual menu — every banchan, every BBQ cut, every stew identified by image — which removes the language barrier entirely.
