FAQ

Questions merchants ask us.

All FAQs
What's included in the ShapesPay POS bundle?

The full bundle pairs the D3 smart register (with its built-in receipt printer), a wireless Moneris Go Plus terminal, and an integrated Moneris Go PIN Pad — all connected to your ShapesPay merchant portal. Onboarding, staff training and 24/7 support are part of every setup.

Can I start with just a terminal and add the register later?

Absolutely. The system is modular — many merchants start with a single wireless terminal and add the register, PIN pad, or extra terminals as they grow. Everything you add reports into the same portal.

Which payment types can I accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Interac debit — by tap, chip insert, or swipe — plus mobile wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay. Card-not-present sales for phone and mail orders are supported through manual entry, and gift card programs are available.

Do the devices need an internet connection?

Yes — the register and PIN pad connect over your Wi-Fi or wired Ethernet, while the Go Plus terminal runs on 4G cellular out of the box and switches to Wi-Fi automatically whenever your network signal is stronger. That means the wireless terminal keeps working even if your store internet drops.

How do refunds and end-of-day work?

Refunds and voids take a few taps from the Reports screen on the terminal — you can even scan the QR code on a receipt to pull up the exact transaction. Your batch closes automatically each night, funds settle to your business account, and totals appear in your portal reporting.

How long does setup take?

Most merchants are live within days. We pre-configure your hardware, help build your product catalog, connect the devices to your network, and train your team — then stay available around the clock once you're selling.

What are my payment options for POS plans?

Hardware is a simple monthly rental, per device, billed monthly — no long lock-in, cancel anytime. Software and processing are quoted to fit your business, and bundled setups can be combined on a single bill.

Are there any upfront costs?

No hidden setup fees. Because hardware is rented monthly, there's no large equipment purchase up front. If your setup needs custom work, we quote it clearly before you commit to anything.

How much does support cost?

Nothing extra. Onboarding, staff training and 24/7 phone, email and chat support are included with every ShapesPay plan — you're our customer, so we look after you.

Can I use my own card processor?

ShapesPay handles your card and Interac processing end-to-end on Moneris — one of Canada's payment leaders — with transparent rates quoted to your monthly volume. That's what keeps your POS, payments and reporting together in one place, on one statement.

Will it work for franchises or multiple locations?

Yes. The merchant portal is built for multi-location businesses — switch between locations, manage menus and users centrally, and see consolidated reports across every terminal and store from one dashboard.
