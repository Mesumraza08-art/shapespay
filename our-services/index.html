Our Payment Solutions

ShapesPay provides a complete suite of modern payment solutions designed to support businesses at every stage — from in-store transactions to mobile payments and online commerce. Our services are built to be secure, scalable, and easy to manage, helping businesses streamline operations and deliver a seamless payment experience to their customers.

Handheld Terminal Rentals

Accept payments anywhere with portable, secure handheld terminals.

ShapesPay’s handheld terminal rentals are ideal for businesses that operate on the move or require flexible payment solutions. Designed for reliability and ease of use, our Android-powered terminals enable fast, secure transactions wherever your business operates.

Key Capabilities

  • Portable Android smart terminals
  • Tap, Chip & PIN, Interac, Apple Pay, and Google Pay
  • Wi-Fi and 4G connectivity for uninterrupted transactions
  • Digital receipts via email or SMS

Use Cases

Perfect for businesses that require mobility, faster checkout times, and a modern payment experience without long-term hardware commitments.

Ideal for mobile businesses, salons, cafés, delivery services, and events

POS Systems (In-Store Solutions)

Power your in-store operations with reliable, scalable POS systems.

ShapesPay’s POS systems are designed for retail stores, restaurants, clinics, and multi-location businesses that require dependable in-store payment processing. Our hardware and software work together to deliver speed, security, and centralized control across all locations.

Ideal for businesses seeking faster transactions, improved customer experience, and scalable payment infrastructure

Ideal for retail stores, restaurants, clinics, and multi-location businesses

  • Smart countertop POS terminals
  • Chip, PIN, and contactless payments
  • Optimized Interac routing for higher approval rates
  • Centralized dashboard for multi-location management
  • Advanced sales, payment, and performance reporting
  • PCI DSS compliant security standards

Ecommerce Payment Gateway

Enable fast, secure online payments with a powerful ecommerce gateway.

ShapesPay’s ecommerce payment gateway allows businesses to accept payments online with confidence. Whether you operate an online store, subscription-based service, or digital platform, our gateway integrates seamlessly into your existing systems.

Designed for ecommerce businesses, SaaS platforms, healthcare providers, and service-based companies requiring flexible online payment solutions.

Ideal for online stores, subscription services, and digital platforms

  • Secure hosted checkout
  • Recurring billing and subscription management
  • Payment links for email, SMS, and messaging platforms
  • Developer-friendly APIs for custom integrations
  • WooCommerce plugin for fast setup
  • Support for credit cards, Interac, and digital wallets
