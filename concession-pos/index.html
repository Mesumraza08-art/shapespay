Inventory Management Made Simple.

When you're moving hundreds — or thousands — of hot dogs and burgers per event, staying on top of stock isn't optional. ShapesPay's smart Inventory Management tracks at the ingredient level, in real time, so you never run out of what you need mid-event. Set low-stock alerts, manage every concessionaire and supplier from one dashboard, and auto-generate purchase orders before the next game night.

Mobile Line-Busting

Go Mobile. Break The Lines In The Concourse.

You may have a fixed number of stations in the arena, but that doesn't mean a fixed number of POS terminals. ShapesPay's wireless tablets let staff take the till to the crowd — out into the concourse, the bowl, or the upper-level corridors. Bust the lines, take orders anywhere, print pickup tickets, and direct fans to the pickup area. More throughput per stand, fewer fans missing the third quarter.
