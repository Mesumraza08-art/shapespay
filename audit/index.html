Content audit
& page map

Every public URL on shapespay.com (177 pages and posts crawled on November 5, 2026) mapped to its page in the redesign, with the items that need an owner decision before launch.

Review before launch

12 items flagged

Nothing below was changed or invented. Conflicts are shown as found on the live site.

Homepage

Pricing conflict

Homepage pricing card reads "POS Rental, From $150 /mo" while the itemized list on the same card starts at $22/mo (Moneris V400c) and tops out at $99/mo (kiosk). The redesign shows only the itemized rates. Confirm what "$150/mo" refers to before launch.

Home, POS Solution, In-Store

Uptime figure

Homepage and POS Solution say 99.9% uptime; the In-Store POS Hardware page says "99.99% uptime". Both kept where they appear. Pick one figure.

Homepage

Counters

Live homepage counters render as "0 %" and "0 +" until JavaScript runs. Their target values (95%, 1000+, 99.9%) are used in the redesign.

Home + industry pages

Testimonials

The same three names (Michael Anderson, Sarah Collins, David Reynolds) appear on the homepage and ~90 industry pages, each time with a different, industry-specific quote. Kept exactly as published. Confirm these are approved, real customer statements before launch.

Industry pages

Industry claims

Industry pages include volume claims such as "Hundreds of liquor and wine stores across Canada" and "Thousands of merchants". Kept verbatim; verify.

In-Store

HIPAA wording

In-Store POS Hardware lists "HIPAA-compliant payment solutions" for clinics. HIPAA is a US law; Canadian clinics fall under PIPEDA/provincial rules. Kept; review wording.

Ecommerce Gateway

Conversion stat

Ecommerce Gateway says checkout flows "increase completion rates by up to 30%". Kept; needs a source or removal.

Legal

Legal boilerplate

/privacy-policy/ and /terms-and-conditions/ contain WordPress "Suggested text:" placeholder privacy copy (the Terms page is not terms). Both now 301 to Privacy & Legal, which holds the real Privacy, Terms, Cookies, Disclaimer and AODA sections.

All quote forms

Forms

Quote and contact forms are styled front-ends. The live site uses a LeadConnector (GoHighLevel) embed; wire the new forms to it, or drop the embed in, at launch.

Global nav

Brief cut off

The brief file ends at "Navigation structure / Left". Navigation follows the standard pattern: logo left, Solutions and Your Business menus, Pricing, About, Blog, Switch, yellow Get Started.

Industry pages

Orphan pages

17 industry pages (e.g. Pizzeria, Fine Dining, Brewery, Nail Salon, Laundry, Gun Store) exist in the sitemap but are not linked from the live parent pages. Each is now linked from its closest parent and from sibling pages.

About

Team photos

Ali Abbas (placeholder image) and David Macereth (no image) have no photos on the live About page; monograms are shown.
